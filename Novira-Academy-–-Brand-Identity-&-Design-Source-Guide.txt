# Official Color Schema

## Primary Colors

* Deep Royal Purple → `#5B14E6`
* Electric Violet → `#7B2CFF`
* Bright Blue Accent → `#2196FF`

## Secondary Colors

* Soft White → `#F8F8FA`
* Light Gray Background → `#ECECEC`
* Dark Purple Shadow → `#2A0A66`

## Gradient Style

Always use:

* Purple → Blue gradients
* Soft glow gradients
* Neon premium lighting effects

Example:

* `linear-gradient(135deg, #5B14E6 → #2196FF)`

---

# Typography Style

## Arabic Headlines

* Bold
* Large
* 3D or semi-3D effect
* Strong shadows
* White glow or purple glow

## English Text

* Clean sans-serif
* Modern spacing
* Premium minimal appearance

# Logo Rules

## Logo Usage

* Always use original Novira Academy logo
* Keep logo clean with enough spacing
* Use white/purple backgrounds only
* Never distort logo proportions

## Logo Position

Preferred:

* Top left
* Clean spacing around logo

# Background Style

## Preferred Backgrounds

* Soft white modern backgrounds
* Purple abstract gradients
* Blurred educational buildings
* Premium light effects
* Subtle geometric shapes

* Cheap clipart
* Random colors
* Dark messy backgrounds

# Lighting & Effects

## Use

* Soft shadows

* Smooth depth

* Cheap filters
* Excessive effects
