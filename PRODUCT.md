# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Egyptian students (ages 14-22) and their parents seeking:
- Professional development and education programs
- Certified translation services
- American diploma programs
- International educational opportunities

Context: Parents researching for their children; students exploring career paths. Primary motivation: trust, accreditation, and proven success outcomes.

## Product Purpose

Novira Academy is a multi-department educational institution in Egypt offering three core services:
1. Education & Professional Development
2. Certified Translation Services
3. American Diploma Programs

Success means: prospective students enroll, parents feel confident choosing Novira, and the institution is perceived as an established, internationally accredited educational provider.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, Documentary, Aspirational

Voice: Institutional confidence without arrogance. Documentary authenticity over promotional hype. Aspirational yet grounded in real outcomes.

Emotional goals: Trust first, then ambition. Parents should feel relief ("this is the right choice"). Students should feel possibility ("this is where my future begins").

## Anti-references

Do NOT make this look like:
- A generic SaaS startup landing page
- A cheap local training center
- An AI-generated template with random gradients and floating shapes
- A university website from the 2000s
- A luxury brand focused on aesthetics over credibility
- A page overloaded with icons, illustrations, and decorative graphics

Avoid:
- Fake stock photos
- Empty marketing buzzwords
- Excessive animations
- Cluttered layouts
- Too many colors
- Card grids everywhere
- Overdesigned glassmorphism

The design should feel like a real, established educational institution with authentic student life, documented achievements, and international credibility.

It should feel documentary, institutional, and trustworthy before it feels promotional.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust over decoration**: Every element must earn its place through credibility, not aesthetics. Accreditation logos, student testimonials, and documented achievements carry more weight than gradient backgrounds.

2. **Documentary authenticity**: Real student life, real outcomes, real institutions. No fake stock photos, no empty promises. The design documents success, not promises it.

3. **Institutional confidence**: Premium without being luxury. Modern without being trendy. The design should feel established and permanent, not like a startup finding its footing.

4. **RTL-first clarity**: Arabic is the primary language. Layout, typography, and spacing must serve RTL readers first, with English as a secondary language.

5. **Mobile-first Egyptian audience**: Optimized for Facebook sharing and mobile browsing in Egypt. Fast loading, clear hierarchy, accessible on all devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA with selective AAA enhancements:
- Full keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Semantic HTML structure
- Proper ARIA labels
- Accessible forms with clear validation
- Sufficient color contrast (AA minimum, AAA for large text)
- Screen reader compatibility
- RTL accessibility support

Apply AAA where it provides high value:
- Large text contrast for headings
- Form usability for registration flows
- Button and link clarity
- Reading experience for educational content

Do not sacrifice visual design or user experience in pursuit of full AAA compliance. The goal is a modern educational institution website that is highly accessible, practical, and production-ready.
