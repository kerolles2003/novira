# SPRINT 4.2.W — The Website, Rewritten as the Academy Actually Operates

**Author's stance:** written by the Academic Director after one week inside Novira — watching level assessments, weekly classes, teachers explaining the American curriculum, homework, mock exams, the registration desk, travel arrangements, and exam day.

**The single test applied to every heading, paragraph, CTA, card, FAQ, metric, testimonial, and stepper:**
> *Would a parent who spent a week inside Novira recognise this as the real service?*
> If **No** → rewritten to describe the process, not the destination.

**The service the whole site must describe (the only thing we sell):**
assessment → choosing the correct exam → weekly classes → teachers explaining the curriculum → homework → practice → mock exams → continuous academic follow-up → registration support → travel coordination when required → the official exam → helping the student reach the highest possible score.

**Banned from the site** (destination/hype language): "تبني مستقبلك", "نحو القبول الجامعي", "باب التميز", "كن أنت التالي", "مسارات", "future-ready", leadership, "unlock opportunities". **Replaced by** the classroom: teachers, weekly schedule, homework, revision, mock exams, follow-up, parent updates, registration, travel, exam day, score improvement.

---

## GOVERNING PRINCIPLE (v2) — activity → outcome → score

The parent is not buying assessment, homework, registration, or travel. The parent is buying **a student who becomes academically ready and walks into the official exam capable of the highest possible score.** That is the value. The operation is only *how* we deliver it.

Therefore two laws govern every heading, paragraph, card, metric, CTA, FAQ, and stepper on the site:

**Law 1 — Never describe an isolated activity. Always connect it to its educational outcome, and let that outcome point at the score.**
- حصص أسبوعية → *لبناء الفهم*
- واجبات وتدريب → *لترسيخ التعلّم وتحويله إلى مهارة*
- اختبارات تجريبية → *لقياس الجاهزية*
- متابعة مستمرة → *لتحسين نقاط الضعف ورفع المستوى*
- دعم التسجيل → *لتخفيف العبء الإداري عن الأسرة*
- التنسيق اللوجستي (عند الحاجة) → *لتجربة اختبار مريحة*
Every sentence must be answerable by: **"How does this help the student reach a higher score?"** If a sentence can't answer that, it is rewritten or cut.

**Law 2 — The journey does not stop at "the official exam." The climax is the *prepared student*:**
> *"يدخل طالبك الامتحان الرسمي مستعداً فعلاً، قادراً على تحقيق أفضل درجة ممكنة."*
The exam is the moment; **readiness and the highest possible score are the value.** No section may end on a mechanical "then he sits the exam" — it ends on *why the weeks of work mean he sits it ready*.

**Value-proposition hierarchy** (order of prominence across the site):
1. Qualified, specialised teachers
2. Structured weekly classes
3. Continuous academic follow-up
4. Practice & homework
5. Mock exams
6. Performance & score improvement
7. Registration assistance
→ **Travel coordination is a supporting operational service, not a headline value.** It appears only where context requires it (an exam-registration/logistics block), never in heroes, primary cards, or value grids.

---

## HOW THE SITE SHOULD NARRATE THE JOURNEY

Every page is a viewport onto the same operation — but framed as value, not mechanics. The homepage shows the whole arc *climaxing in a ready student*; the hub explains it; each exam page shows it for one exam. Nothing describes an outcome we do not control (a university seat). Everything describes work we do, weekly, and *why each piece raises the score*.
